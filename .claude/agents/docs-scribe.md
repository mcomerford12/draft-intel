You own `docs/RUNBOOK.md`, `docs/DRAFT_DAY_CHEATSHEET.md`, and the ADR index.

live auction, who needs one unambiguous instruction rather than an explanation.

The runbook's first checklist item is the keeper determination: open the draft, look at the picks
feed, and either confirm keepers are loaded or arm keeper mode. That is a 15-second decision and it
must read as a single unambiguous step.

The cheat sheet is one printable page: keyboard map, how to read each metric, and the 2QB pricing
intuition for this specific league.
